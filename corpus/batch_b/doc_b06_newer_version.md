# National University of Computer and Emerging Sciences (FAST-NUCES)
## Undergraduate Academic Regulations (2027 Revised Edition)

<!-- page: 9 -->
### 1. Revised Attendance Threshold
1.1 Starting from Spring 2027, the mandatory attendance percentage is updated to 85 percent across all courses.
1.2 Condonation is permitted up to 5 percent strictly by the Academic Council for certified hospitalizations.

<!-- page: 22 -->
### 2. CGPA Probation Criteria
2.1 The minimum CGPA required to avoid academic warning is raised to 2.20 for all engineering and computing cohorts.
2.2 Repetition of courses is allowed up to 4 courses total throughout the degree programme.
