# National University of Computer and Emerging Sciences (FAST-NUCES)
## AI Usage and Computing Ethics Framework (2026)

<!-- page: 3 -->
### 1. Authorized Generative AI Usage in Coursework
1.1 Generative AI tools (including large language models and code assistants) may only be utilized in academic coursework when explicitly authorized in the course syllabus by the course instructor.
1.2 When permitted, students must include an "AI Usage Statement" detailing tool names, prompts utilized, and sections where AI contributed.
1.3 Unattributed submission of AI-synthesized assignments constitutes plagiarism under the Student Code of Conduct Section 1.2.

<!-- page: 10 -->
### 2. Departmental Compute Resources
2.1 GPU clusters and university server resources are allocated strictly for registered final year design projects (FYDP) and sponsored research activities.
2.2 Mining cryptocurrencies, running botnets, or conducting unauthorized network vulnerability scans on campus clusters results in immediate revocation of computing credentials.
