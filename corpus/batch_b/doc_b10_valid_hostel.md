# National University of Computer and Emerging Sciences (FAST-NUCES)
## Residential Hostel and Campus Transport Regulations (2026)

<!-- page: 5 -->
### 1. Hostel Allotment and Curfew Hours
1.1 Hostel accommodation is allotted on a merit-cum-distance basis to full-time registered undergraduate and graduate students.
1.2 The mandatory curfew time for all residential halls is 10:00 PM on weekdays and 11:00 PM on weekends. Late entry requires prior written approval from the Resident Warden.
1.3 Subletting hostel rooms to unauthorized individuals or non-students incurs immediate expulsion from student housing and forfeiture of hostel security deposit.

<!-- page: 14 -->
### 2. University Transport Service
2.1 Point buses operate on designated city routes during morning and evening rush hours for registered semester bus pass holders.
2.2 Tampering with route cards or sharing transport credentials with unregistered individuals results in a fine of PKR 5,000 and one-month suspension of transport privileges.
