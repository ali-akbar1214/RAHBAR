# National University of Computer and Emerging Sciences (FAST-NUCES)
## Undergraduate Prospectus & Academic Programmes Overview (2026)

<!-- page: 5 -->
### 1. Campuses and Degree Programmes
1.1 FAST-NUCES operates five established campuses across Pakistan: Islamabad, Lahore, Karachi, Peshawar, and Chiniot-Faisalabad (CFD).
1.2 The Department of Software Engineering offers specialized curricula in modern software architecture, quality engineering, verification, cloud computing, and DevOps.

<!-- page: 18 -->
### 2. Grading Scheme and SGPA / CGPA Calculation
2.1 Grade points are awarded on a four-point scale: A (4.00), A- (3.67), B+ (3.33), B (3.00), B- (2.67), C+ (2.33), C (2.00), C- (1.67), D+ (1.33), D (1.00), F (0.00).
2.2 The Semester Grade Point Average (SGPA) is computed as the sum of products of credit hours and grade points divided by the total credit hours registered in the semester:
    SGPA = Sum(Credit_Hours * Grade_Points) / Sum(Credit_Hours).
2.3 The Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) represents the weighted average of grade points earned in all completed courses across all semesters.
