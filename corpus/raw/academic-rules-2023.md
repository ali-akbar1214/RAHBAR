# National University of Computer and Emerging Sciences (FAST-NUCES)
## Undergraduate Academic Regulations (2023 Edition - Superseded Policy)

*Notice: This edition is superseded by the 2026 Regulations and is maintained for historical archival and rollback verification.*

<!-- page: 4 -->
### 1. Attendance Regulations (Historical 2023)
1.1 Under the 2023 academic rules, a student must attend at least 75 percent of scheduled class lectures and lab sessions to sit for the final exam. (Note: the 2026 edition raised this threshold to 80 percent).
1.2 A student falling below the 75 percent requirement received the grade FA.

<!-- page: 12 -->
### 2. Academic Warning & Probation (Historical 2023)
2.1 A student whose CGPA dropped below 1.75 was placed on Academic Warning at the end of a regular semester.
2.2 A student remaining on warning for two consecutive semesters was dismissed from the university (academic drop).

<!-- page: 19 -->
### 3. Course Repetition Policy (Historical 2023)
3.1 A student was eligible to repeat any course in which their earned grade was C or lower.
3.2 In the 2023 policy, when a course was repeated to improve CGPA, the maximum permissible grade on the repeated attempt was capped at B (3.00 grade points).
