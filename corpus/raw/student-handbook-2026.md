# National University of Computer and Emerging Sciences (FAST-NUCES)
## Student Handbook & Institutional Code of Conduct (2026 Edition)

<!-- page: 4 -->
### 1. Code of Academic Integrity & Plagiarism
1.1 All students are expected to adhere strictly to the highest standards of academic honesty in all examinations, laboratory projects, assignments, and semester research papers.
1.2 Plagiarism is defined as the intentional or unintentional presentation of another individual's work, code, concepts, or textual phrasing as one's own without appropriate citation and attribution.
1.3 In Software Development and Computer Science courses, source code copying from peers, public repositories, or generative models without authorization constitutes serious academic dishonesty.

<!-- page: 11 -->
### 2. Disciplinary Actions & Penalties
2.1 When an instructor or university integrity committee confirms an infraction of academic dishonesty or plagiarism:
   - First minor offense: The student receives an immediate failing grade "F" in the respective assignment or course, accompanied by an official written reprimand placed in the permanent record.
   - Second or severe offense: The Academic Disciplinary Committee shall suspend the student's registration for one or two regular semesters.
   - Third offense: Permanent expulsion from FAST-NUCES across all campuses without possibility of re-admission.
2.2 When a student's registration is suspended, they are barred from attending classes, entering university premises, utilizing laboratory workstations, or participating in campus extracurricular activities for the duration of the suspension.

<!-- page: 18 -->
### 3. Campus Conduct, Dress Code, and Property
3.1 Smoking, vaping, and possession of illicit substances anywhere on campus premises, including cafeterias and parking lots, is strictly prohibited and subject to immediate fine and disciplinary review.
3.2 Willful destruction, tampering, or unauthorized reconfiguration of university laboratory hardware, network infrastructure, or library property results in monetary restitution and potential semester suspension.
