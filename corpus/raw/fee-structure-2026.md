# National University of Computer and Emerging Sciences (FAST-NUCES)
## Official Schedule of Tuition Fees & Financial Policies (2026-2027)

<!-- page: 2 -->
### 1. Undergraduate & Graduate Tuition Rates
1.1 For undergraduate programmes (BS in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, Cyber Security, and Electrical Engineering):
    - Tuition Fee per Credit Hour: PKR 10,000.
    - Regular 15-credit semester total tuition: PKR 150,000.
    - Admission Fee (one-time non-refundable): PKR 30,000.
    - Security Deposit (refundable upon degree clearance): PKR 20,000.
1.2 For Master of Science (MS) programmes:
    - Tuition Fee per Credit Hour: PKR 12,500.
1.3 For Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) programmes:
    - Full fee waiver and research stipend subject to university research committee award.

<!-- page: 6 -->
### 2. Incidental Fees & Service Charges
2.1 Late Course Registration Surcharge: PKR 3,000 per week after official deadline.
2.2 Official Academic Transcript (per attested physical copy): PKR 1,500.
2.3 Inter-Campus Transfer Processing Fee: PKR 5,000.
2.4 Degree Certificate Verification Fee: PKR 2,500.

<!-- page: 9 -->
### 3. Fee Refund Schedule upon Course or Programme Withdrawal
3.1 If a student formally drops a course or withdraws from the semester within the first ten (10) calendar days of the start of classes: 100 percent of tuition fees refunded.
3.2 If withdrawal occurs between day 11 and day 20 of the semester: 50 percent of tuition fees refunded.
3.3 After day 20 of classes: Zero (0) percent refund; the student forfeits all tuition fees for that semester. Admission fee is strictly non-refundable under all circumstances.
